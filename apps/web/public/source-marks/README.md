# Source marks

These marks identify the external data and publication sources linked from the
Forudid about page. They do not imply endorsement or affiliation. Display is
monochrome and size-only; the source artwork is otherwise unchanged.

- `sentinel-1.png`: ESA Sentinel-1 mission logotype, downloaded from the
  [ESA Earth Observation Mission Logotypes](https://www.esa.int/eologos/sentinel-1.html) page and converted from JPG to transparent PNG.
- `zenodo.svg`: Zenodo's production header mark from
  `https://zenodo.org/static/images/invenio-rdm.svg`.
- `science-advances.png`: Science Advances wordmark from the transparent asset
  published by Seeklogo, trimmed without redrawing.
- `openstreetmap.svg`: official OpenStreetMap logo from the
  [OpenStreetMap logo catalogue](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Logos).
- `asf.png`: Alaska Satellite Facility logo served by the header of its
  [official website](https://asf.alaska.edu/).
- `gfz.svg`: current GFZ wordmark from the GFZ media asset mirrored on
  [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:GFZLogoGer.svg).
- `agu.svg`: American Geophysical Union wordmark from
  [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:American_Geophysical_Union_logo.svg).
- `geofabrik.png`: Geofabrik symbol served by its
  [official website](https://www.geofabrik.de/).

All names and marks remain the property of their respective owners. Each mark
links to the source, publication, or documentation it identifies.
