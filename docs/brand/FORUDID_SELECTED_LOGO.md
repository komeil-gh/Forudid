# Selected FORUDID mark

## Approved version

The selected mark is the black first concept: a polygonal ring frame, central ring, diagonal white gap and stepped lower detail. Later color explorations must not replace its shape or color.

## Files

- Transparent original: `apps/web/public/brand/selected/forudid-mark-black.png`
- White-background preview: `apps/web/public/brand/selected/forudid-mark-black-preview.png`
- Reference construction sheet: `docs/brand/studies/forudid-concept-01-construction.png`

## Specifications and use

The original is 1024 × 1024 with real transparency, square proportions and pure black `#000000`. Its diagonal gap, rings and stepped lower detail are fixed. Use the transparent original on light backgrounds. Add no color, gradient, shadow, frame or effect. Preserve proportions, gap angle and lower geometry. The color explorations in `brand/v3` were rejected and are not the selected version.

## About-letter watermark

At the user's request, the mark appears at 4.5% opacity over white behind the letter. This gray appearance is only a decorative watermark; the original asset and black header mark remain unchanged.
